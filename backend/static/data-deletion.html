Account & Data Deletion Request

AI Crypstock Pro — Last updated: July 2026

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. This page explains how.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to cryptostockapps@gmail.com with subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include in the email body:
    • Your registered email address (the email you used to log in to the app)
    • Your device ID (optional — helps us identify your account faster)
  3. Verification: We may reply asking you to confirm the request from the same email address (to prevent unauthorized deletion).
  4. Deletion completed: Within 30 days of verified request, all your personal data will be removed from our systems.
📧 Send Deletion Request Now

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your email address and unlock code record
  • Your device binding (device ID)
  • Your subscription record (from our backend — Google Play retains its own record per their policy)
  • Any AI analysis session data linked to your account

What Data Will NOT Be Deleted

  • Payment / subscription records managed by Google Play — you must request deletion separately via Google Play Support. We do not have access to your payment data.
  • Anonymized aggregated analytics (e.g., total app installs, crash statistics) that cannot be linked back to you.
  • Legal / financial records we are required to retain by law (e.g., tax records, dispute logs) — retained per applicable regulations, then permanently deleted.

Partial Deletion (Optional)

If you want to delete only some data (e.g., unbind your device but keep your account), specify that in your email. We accept partial deletion requests.
